How it actually goes

From inbound message to delivered edit

No black box. Here's exactly what happens after you reach out.

01 — Inbound

Work comes in

A call, a DM, an email — someone needs an editor or a hand with their socials. I get back to you fast, not in three business days.

02 — Scope

I jump on it

I find out exactly what the channel needs — a single edit, a full posting calendar, or everything in between — and scope it to fit, not a fixed package.

Check-ins, revisions, footage turning into something that actually sounds like your channel — not a template applied to your clips.

Finished pieces ship matched to what you actually asked for — then we refine the process together as the channel keeps growing.
